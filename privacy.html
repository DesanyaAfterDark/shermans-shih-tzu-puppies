Privacy policy

This page covers the litter inquiry form on the contact page.

What the form collects

The form asks for your name, your email address, and your message.

Where it is sent

The message is emailed to greenpea@live.com so Shannon Sherman can reply about a visit, a free Zoom viewing, or the wait list. We do not sell this information.

Ask us to remove it

Email greenpea@live.com and ask us to delete the message you sent. We will remove it from the mailbox we can still reach.
