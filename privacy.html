Privacy policy

This page covers the litter inquiry, the visit request, and the review form. The first two are on the booking page. The review form is on the home page.

What the forms collect

The inquiry form asks for your name, your email address, and your message. The visit request also asks for your phone number, a preferred date, a preferred time of day, how many people are coming, which puppy you hope to meet, and a message if you wish to add one. The review form asks for your name, a star rating, and your review. An email address on that form is optional.

Where it is sent

The message is emailed to greenpea@live.com so Shannon Sherman can reply about a visit, a FaceTime visit, the wait list, or a review. A visit request and a review are also copied to desanyaafterdark@gmail.com. A review is posted on this site only after we approve it. We do not sell this information.

Ask us to remove it

Email greenpea@live.com and ask us to delete the message you sent. We will remove it from the mailbox we can still reach.
